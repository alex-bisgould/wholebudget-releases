First-time setup

  1. Open the downloaded WholeBudget-mac-arm64.dmg.
  2. Drag WholeBudget.app into your Applications folder — that's the whole install.
  3. Open WholeBudget. It starts empty; add your accounts, then import a statement file or enter your first month by hand.
Your data is yours. Everything you enter — accounts, transactions, receipts, bills, history — lives in ~/Library/Application Support/WholeBudget, outside the app itself. Updating or even deleting and reinstalling the app never touches it, and it is included in any normal Time Machine backup.

Signed and notarized by Apple — it opens like any other Mac app, no warnings and no Terminal.

Requirements

  1. A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 13 or later.
  2. Roughly 600 MB of disk space, plus room for your own receipts.
